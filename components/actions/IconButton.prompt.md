Icon-only button (close, prev/next, favorite, social); always pass `label`.
```jsx
<IconButton label="Cerrar" tone="dark"><Icon name="x" /></IconButton>
<IconButton label="Favorito" variant="solid" active><Icon name="heart" size={16} /></IconButton>
```
- Variants: outline (default), round, solid (blurred chip over photos), plain.
